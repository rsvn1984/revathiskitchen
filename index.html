About Revathi's Kitchen

Revathi's Kitchen was born from Revathi's long-held dream of bringing authentic, traditional Brahmin-style homemade food to our customers.

Established on 10.06.2026, we prepare food with homemade ingredients, traditional methods and a strong focus on taste and hygiene.

We especially believe in simple, wholesome food without heavy masalas, commercially branded masala powders or artificial food colours — food that elders and families can enjoy with confidence.
