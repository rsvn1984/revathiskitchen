About Us

The Story Behind Revathi's Kitchen

The idea of creating a cloud kitchen had been in Revathi's mind for many years.

A traditional Vaikhanasa homemaker, Revathi has always believed that good food should be simple, authentic, tasty and prepared with care.

This long-held idea finally became a reality when Revathi's Kitchen was established on 10.06.2026.

Our Simple Principle

At Revathi's Kitchen, we follow a simple principle — food that is gentle on the palate, especially for elders.

We avoid heavy masalas, commercially branded masala powders and artificial food colours. We strive to use homemade ingredients wherever possible and prepare every dish with attention to taste, hygiene and traditional methods.

Traditional Food, Prepared Traditionally

We aim to serve customers who are looking for food prepared in the traditional style of a Brahmin household kitchen.

Without compromising on our principles, we prepare our food using authentic traditional methods, preserving the distinctive taste and character of this style of cooking.

Our Customers

With the blessings of God and the continued support of our customers, a significant part of our customer base today consists of elders, people looking for traditional Brahmin-style home food, and those who prefer food without heavy masalas and excessive spices.

Our Vision

We look forward to expanding our customer base while continuing to provide authentic Brahmin-style food, preserving its traditional character, unique taste and simplicity.
